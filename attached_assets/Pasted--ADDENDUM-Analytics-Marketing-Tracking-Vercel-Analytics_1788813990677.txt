## 3. PRIVACY / CONSENT CONSIDERATION

Since this is a public portfolio that may receive visitors from the EU/UK (where GDPR/consent rules for analytics cookies apply) as well as elsewhere:

1. Add a simple, unobtrusive **cookie/analytics consent notice** (a small banner, dismissible, appearing on first visit) informing visitors that analytics are used, with an "Accept"/"Decline" choice — and make Google Analytics only actually fire *after* consent is given (Vercel's own analytics is generally considered more privacy-friendly and typically doesn't require the same consent gating, but Google Analytics does, since it sets identifying cookies).
2. Keep this lightweight and consistent with the site's visual design (rounded, matching the established button/color system) — it should not feel like a jarring, generic cookie-law popup.
3. If a full custom consent solution feels like overkill for a personal portfolio, propose the simplest defensible approach and explain the trade-off to Ines plainly, rather than silently skipping consent handling altogether.
